# AI_TRG_FLEX

## AI Training Resources - FLEX System

Welcome to the AI Training Resources for FLEX! This repository contains comprehensive training materials, tutorials, and best practices for working with AI systems in the FLEX environment.

## 📚 Overview

This training program is designed to help developers, data scientists, and AI practitioners understand and effectively implement AI solutions within the FLEX framework. Whether you're just starting out or looking to advance your skills, you'll find valuable resources here.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By completing this training, you will:
- Understand the fundamentals of AI in the FLEX environment
- Learn best practices for AI model development and deployment
- Gain hands-on experience with practical examples
- Master advanced techniques for optimization and scaling
- Develop skills in AI ethics and responsible AI development
